About Me

I help businesses turn data into decisions.. and decisions into measurable outcomes.

My journey started in Zimbabwe, matured in South Africa’s banking industry, and sharpened in the United States across finance, analytics, and business systems. That global path taught me how to navigate complexity, adapt quickly, and deliver meaningful results in fast-moving environments.

Today, I specialize in bridging finance, analytics, and operations to help organizations improve visibility, strengthen decision-making, and scale more effectively. My work focuses on FP&A, operational analytics, executive reporting, forecasting, profitability analysis, and building systems that leadership teams can confidently rely on.

I’ve contributed across healthcare, education, and retail organizations by building scalable reporting processes, improving financial visibility, and connecting operational data to strategic business outcomes — from pharmacy reconciliation and grant tracking to pricing analysis, store-level profitability modeling, and enterprise reporting automation.

What I bring to the table:

  • Strategic finance and FP&A support across operational and executive teams
  • Hands-on experience with SQL, Excel, Snowflake, Power BI, Tableau, QuickBooks, and EHR systems
  • Executive dashboards and reporting workflows that reduced reporting turnaround time by 40%
  • Forecasting, budgeting, and profitability models used to support planning and growth decisions
  • Grant tracking and AR workflow improvements that recovered $250K+ while saving 15+ hours monthly
  • Cross-functional collaboration with CFOs, COOs, Operations, BI, and Accounting leadership
  • Operational analytics and process improvement initiatives that strengthened controls and business visibility
  • Data storytelling that translates complex information into actionable business direction

In my first 30-60 days, I focus on identifying reporting blind spots, improving forecast reliability, strengthening operational visibility, and creating data stories leadership can confidently act on.
